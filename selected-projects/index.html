Selected Projects

Selected research outputs on AI, journalism, information systems, and public accountability.

  1. CNTI · Research briefing · 2026

    Internet Shutdowns, Press Freedom and the Global Information Crisis: Iran as a Case Study

    Examines how internet restrictions in Iran affect reporting, access to information, and the ability to verify events, with attention to inequalities in connectivity and the spread of misinformation.

  2. CNTI · Issue primer · 2026

    Addressing “Lawful but Awful” Content

    Surveys the difficulty of governing legal but harmful online content, including tradeoffs among free expression, platform moderation, journalistic evidence, and accountability.

  3. CNTI · Research briefing · 2026

    AI Applications in Investigative Journalism

    Synthesizes research on where AI tools can support investigative reporting and where data quality, source confidentiality, uneven resources, and case-specific methods limit their use.

  4. CNTI · Research briefing · 2026

    Methods for Evaluating AI Tools in Journalism

    Reviews how news providers assess AI tools across different tasks and identifies gaps in journalism-specific evaluation methods, including questions of fit, capacity, and sustainability.
