<ol class="research-list">
  <li class="research-entry">
    <p class="entry-meta">CNTI · Research briefing · 2026</p>
    <h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://cnti.org/briefing/press-freedom-in-iran/">Internet Shutdowns, Press Freedom and the Global Information Crisis: Iran as a Case Study</a></h2>
    <p>Examines how internet restrictions in Iran affect reporting, access to information, and the ability to verify events, with attention to inequalities in connectivity and the spread of misinformation.</p>
  </li>
  <li class="research-entry">
    <p class="entry-meta">CNTI · Issue primer · 2026</p>
    <h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://cnti.org/issue-primers/addressing-lawful-but-awful-content/">Addressing “Lawful but Awful” Content</a></h2>
    <p>Surveys the difficulty of governing legal but harmful online content, including tradeoffs among free expression, platform moderation, journalistic evidence, and accountability.</p>
  </li>
  <li class="research-entry">
    <p class="entry-meta">CNTI · Research briefing · 2026</p>
    <h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://cnti.org/briefing/ai-applications-in-investigative-journalism/">AI Applications in Investigative Journalism</a></h2>
    <p>Synthesizes research on where AI tools can support investigative reporting and where data quality, source confidentiality, uneven resources, and case-specific methods limit their use.</p>
  </li>
  <li class="research-entry">
    <p class="entry-meta">CNTI · Research briefing · 2026</p>
    <h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://cnti.org/briefing/methods-for-evaluating-ai-tools-in-journalism/">Methods for Evaluating AI Tools in Journalism</a></h2>
    <p>Reviews how news providers assess AI tools across different tasks and identifies gaps in journalism-specific evaluation methods, including questions of fit, capacity, and sustainability.</p>
